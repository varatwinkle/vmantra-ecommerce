FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does e-commerce website development cost in India?

Our packages start at ₹19,999 (Starter), ₹34,999 (Business) and ₹49,999+ (Premium). The final cost depends on features and number of products. Use the calculator for an estimate.

Is the calculator price my final quotation?

No. It's an estimate based on your selections. We confirm the final quotation after reviewing your requirements.

How long does it take to build an online store?

It depends on scope and how quickly content is shared. We provide an estimated schedule along with your confirmed quotation.

Which technologies do you use?

WordPress / WooCommerce, custom PHP / MySQL, Laravel and React / Node.js. We help you choose based on budget and long-term plans.

Are domain, hosting and maintenance included?

No, they're optional and shown as separate recurring costs in the calculator. Domain and hosting prices shown are approximate and can vary by provider.

Can I accept online payments and cash on delivery?

Yes. The Business and Premium packages include an online payment gateway and COD. They can be added to Starter as extras.

Will my store rank on Google?

We build an SEO-friendly foundation, but we don't guarantee rankings. Search results depend on many factors outside any developer's control.

Can I manage products myself?

Yes. Every package includes product management, and Business and above include a full admin dashboard.
